## 🗣️ What to Say During Presentation / Defense

When judges at AIU Anveshan ask: *"What is the size and scope of your dataset?"*, here is your exact, structured response:

> *"Our platform combines both **live production telemetry** and a **controlled research benchmark dataset** to evaluate system performance:"*
>
> 1. **Learner State & Telemetry Data**: We have **23 active learner profiles** in our MongoDB Atlas database, tracking user performance across **20 total multimodal assessment attempts** (11 Aptitude, 4 Quiz, 3 Speech, and 2 Coding challenges).
> 2. **Career & Market Intelligence Dataset**: We collected and indexed **176 live job listings** directly from the LinkedIn Jobs API, and our Job Intelligence Agent has semantically parsed **over 50 unstructured job descriptions** to construct real-time market skill frequency matrices.
> 3. **Curriculum & Project Dataset**: We have generated and validated **34 adaptive roadmaps** grounded in skill gap data, as well as **5 full multi-agent hackathon system architecture blueprints**.
> 4. **Controlled Experimental Benchmark**: For our formal comparative study (`Baseline vs. MCP`), we tested **5 distinct learner profiles** across 7 evaluation criteria, generating 35 structured quantitative data points that mathematically confirmed a **+22.9% overall quality improvement** (4.56 vs. 3.71 score) under Model Context Protocol grounding.
